# SuperStore Sales Analytics Dashboard
## 📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing SuperStore sales data and presenting
business insights through an interactive dashboard.
The dashboard provides an overview of sales, profit, orders, shipping
performance, customer segments, payment modes, product categories,
sub-categories, and geographical sales performance.
It also includes a 15-day sales forecast to understand future sales trends.
---
## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Understand sales by customer segment
- Analyze different payment modes
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Analyze sales by sub-category
- Compare monthly sales year-over-year
- Compare monthly profit year-over-year
- Analyze sales across different states
- Study shipping performance
- Generate a 15-day sales forecast
---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis
### Sales Analysis
The dashboard analyzes sales performance across different
segments, categories, sub-categories, payment methods, and states.
### Profit Analysis
Monthly profit trends are analyzed using year-over-year comparisons
to understand changes in profitability.
### Customer Segment Analysis
Sales are divided into:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office
### Payment Mode Analysis
The dashboard analyzes sales based on different payment methods,
including:
- COD
- Online
- Cards
### Product Analysis
Sales performance is analyzed across categories and sub-categories
to identify important product segments.
### Geographical Analysis
Sales are visualized by state to understand geographical sales
distribution.
### Sales Forecast
The project also includes a 15-day sales forecast to analyze
expected future sales trends.
